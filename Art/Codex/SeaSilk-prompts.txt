Sea silk texture set

Generated using built-in image_gen. Final compositing and contact sheets: ImageMagick.
All texture files are 1024x1024 RGBA PNGs. a = single piece; b = folded merchant bolt; c = full roll.
The outer black outline is a 32-pixel radial dilation of the cleaned fill mask at 1024x1024, equivalent to 2 pixels at 64x64, with antialiasing upon downsampling.
Contact sheet icons are each rendered on a 64x64 canvas. The 3x sheet is nearest-neighbor enlargement for inspection.
Vanilla references are unchanged merged composites of the Cloth_a/b/c and Hyperweave_a/b/c PSD files. The white cloth texture is untinted.
All intermediate sources, 64px previews, initial comparison, iteration sheets, and compositing script are in temp/sea-silk-style.
