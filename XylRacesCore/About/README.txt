Xyl's Xeno's 2.0 is a collection of polished, vanilla-like xenotypes. It was originally
released as Posthuman Drift but has recieved a huge amount of rebalancing, enhancements,
and bugfixes, so I'm rereleasing it under the new name.

The collection includes seven xenotypes (with more planned): Bossaps, Chyrr, Dvergr,
Nixie, Titan, Trog, and Warcat. There are also new scenarios, factions, animals, events,
buildings, mental breaks, and of course many new genes, all tied to the new xenotypes in 
some way.

The xenotypes:

Bossaps are genetically engineered as sentient livestock. They can live on a diet of raw
plants, and females produce milk. But beware: if they take damage in combat, they have a
chance of going berserk and rushing the nearest enemy. This gives them signficant stat
boosts, but also takes away your ability to control them until they calm down.

Chyrr are nocturnal, winged bat-people. They can fly for short distances, use
echolocation to see even if their vision is damaged, and stun their enemies with
ultrasonic noise. However, they're quite sensitive to the cold, and even slight drops in
temperature can send them into torpor.

Dvergr are basically angry, alcoholic dwarves. They're good at building, mining, and
crafting. They're happy to stay underground and eat fungus. They're biochemically
dependent on regular alcohol intake. And every single one is a tortured artist.

Nixies are beautiful psychic fish-people. They get innate psycasts, which they can use
even without a psylink, and their tough scales give them built-in damage resistance. But
their semiaquatic nature means they don't do well in hot or dry environments. Thsey're
also sensitive to drugs and very likely to get addicteed.

Titans are engineered as hazardous environment construction workers. Their rock-like skin
functions like armor, and their sheer bulk gives them more hit points than other
xenohumans. They're also resistant to heat, cold, toxins, vacuum, and psychic powers.
The downsides? There are a few...

Trogs are the hideous result of interbreeding between every sort of xenotype across the
rimworlds. They're misshapen and ugly, but surprisingly hard to kill. Worst of all, they
somehow get along with insects, and frequently bring them along on raids.

Warcats are the result of an attempt to create supersoldiers gone wrong. They're vicious
in melee, with razor-sharp claws and incredibly fast reflexes. But their metabolism lacks
the ability to synthesize key nutrients, so they need a steady diet of raw meat to stay
healthy.

Other features:

There's a new system where certain memes agree or disagree with certain xenotypes:
* Bossaps agree with Animal Personhood, Nudism, and Pain is Virtue, and disagree with
  Rancher.
* Chyrr agree with Blindsight.
* Dvergr agree with Tunneler, and disagree with Nature Primacy.
* Nixies agree with High Life and Nudism, and disagreee with Tunneler and Shipborn.
* Titans agree with Shipborn.
* Trogs agree with Flesh Purity, and disagree with Transhumanist.
* Warcats agree with Cannibal and Raider, and disagree with Animal Personhood.
Characters are easier to convert to ideoligeons that agree with their xenotype, and
harder to convert away from one.

There are four new factions:
* Peaceful Nixie Tribe
* Rough Dvergr Union
* Cannibal Warcat Tribe
* Savage Trog Tribe

Nixie traders have a unique pack animal, the selkie, a larger seal that moves faster on
land.

All of the races have distinct visuals:
* Bossaps have cow ears and large horns.
* Chyrr have bat-like wings.
* Dvergr have smaller bodies but full size heads, and small pointed ears.
* Nixies have scaly skin and fin-like ears.
* Titans have large bodies with mottled, rock-like skin.
* Trogs have misshapen heads.
* Warcats have cat ears (the base game ones, sorry), yellow eyes, and facial stripes.

The new genes:
Bossaps: Herd Instinct, Docile, Large Horns, Seeing Red, Pain Reversal, Hyperlactation, Usually Female, Cow Ears, Herbivore Stomach
Chyrr: Sonic Wave, Bat Wings, Torpor, Nocturnal, Keen Ears, Echolocation
Dvergr: Stoic, Melancholy, Small Pointed Ears, Dwarf, Fungus Eater, Industrious
Nixie: Beckon, Focus, Scaleskin, Drug Sensitive, Fin Ears, Aquatic
Titan: Mineralized Skin, Giant, Joyless, Even Temper
Trog: Toxic Burst, Misshapen Head, Bio-Rejection, Genetic Atavism, Insect Pheromones
Warcat: Feral Rage, Moody, Fast Reflexes, Retractable Claws, Yellow Eyes, Facial Stripes, Carnivore Stomach, Meat Dependence

I've done my best to make sure that all the xenotypes are rimworld-y, that they fit in with the base game but also add
something new, and that they will lead to interesting stories.
