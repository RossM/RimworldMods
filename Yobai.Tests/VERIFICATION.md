Earlier runs used visits checked every 250 ticks through `Rand.MTBEventOccurs`, with a mean interval of four in-game hours. That scheduler has since been replaced by the think-tree job giver described below. Seven ideology cases require Ideology, and two child-bedroom tests require Biotech.

The passing runs used `-NoWindow`. An earlier run with `-Headless` logged `Resolution too small (0x0), resetting UI scale to 1.` from `RimWorld.ResolutionUtility.Update`. DevTools failed the active test on that logged error. Its report and game output are in `temp/Yobai-review-headless-*`.

The lovin' tests run through completion and check that both pawns return to sleep without changing bed assignments or sleeping positions. They cover a partner's own bed, a bed both pawns borrow, and a night visit outside the shared bedroom. They now resume vanilla sleep jobs using the bed comp's temporary slot assignments. Separate tests check cancellation and slot reuse, bed access before the sleep job starts, rejection of a booked place taken by another pawn, vanilla sleep without claiming the borrowed bed, queued reservations, the game's rest job giver, and visits to a partner sleeping in someone else's bed.

Guests making night visits require a free unassigned place. Tests cover an absent owner whose assigned place is empty, a host occupying the only unassigned place, and ordinary polycule bedroom sharing that can still use assigned places.

The refactor adds six regression cases: ordinary sleep does not create a temporary assignment; an admitted visit survives its host waking and its toggle changing; incoming guests must be compatible; a rejected second assignment preserves the first stay; automatic sleep cannot claim an ineligible temporary bed; and relationship changes cannot admit incompatible booked bedmates through vanilla owner-based access. The existing cases retain their behavioral checks and now create bookings through the explicit bedroom-sharing or night-visit entry points.

The whole-code review added seven more cases: returning owners must respect current bedmates, vanilla ground-sleep fallback must still consider polycule beds, caravan waiting must not choose beds, medical conversion ends temporary assignments, children may share with their parents' polycule, unrelated children do not qualify, and resurrection restores a bedroom's connecting relationship. The parent case checks freshly calculated vanilla room roles after parent relationships change.

Before those fixes, the returning-owner and ground-fallback cases failed while all 44 previous tests passed. Reports are in `temp/Yobai-domain-review-baseline-*`. The first caravan fixture logged an error because a state added after group initialization lacked its owning group; checking `Lord.SetJob` and `Lord.GotoToil` established the missing initialization. The fixture was corrected without changing its behavioral assertions. That report is in `temp/Yobai-domain-review-dlc-*`.

The review covered all runtime sources, Disharmony patches, XML, project files, test cases, and documentation. It also replaced the default-invalid `SleepingPlace` struct with a validated immutable class, renamed the component and toggle to reflect their responsibilities, and corrected a README encoding error. No changes were made to the unrelated Xylib build error.

The last-resort sharing change adds eight regression cases. They cover a stranded pawn sharing without changing ownership or enabling lovin' with a non-partner; two incoming polycule members booking separate places; empty beds taking priority over incoming non-partners; night visits still requiring direct partners; the exception staying within the assigned bedroom; unrelated sleepers remaining incompatible; strict spouse-only ideoligions allowing fallback sharing; and prohibited lovers retaining vanilla lovin' penalties. The occupied-partner preference test now includes an occupied non-partner bed as a competing choice.

The two ideology fixtures use a four-pawn spouse chain and valid permanent spouse assignments. Shuffling fills one double bed while leaving a place beside an indirect partner in the other. The lovin' case adds a prohibited lover relationship between the fallback bedmates, runs vanilla lovin' through completion, and checks both pawns receive `GotLovin_Abhorrent`, return to sleep, and retain their original bed ownership.

Before the change, the stranded-pawn and incoming-booking regressions failed. An initial ideology fixture also failed, but was invalid: it assigned non-spouses permanently to one bed. Tracing the revised fixture through vanilla `Pawn_IdeoTracker.SetIdeo` and `SpouseRelationUtility.RemoveSpousesAsForbiddenByIdeo` exposed a second setup issue: conversion enforced the generated spouse-count limits and divorced members of the test polycule. The final fixtures explicitly allow unlimited spouses for both genders while retaining strict spouse-only lovin'. These corrections preserve the intended assertions; runtime code has no fixture-specific exceptions. Diagnostic reports are in `temp/Yobai-fallback-baseline-*`, `temp/Yobai-fallback-dlc-*`, and `temp/Yobai-fallback-complete-dlc-*`.

Three existing tests were adjusted for the changed rule: indirect bedmates are now allowed when a bedroom place is explicitly booked, a returning owner still prefers an empty bed, and the incoming-compatibility case rejects an unrelated pawn rather than a same-polycule pawn. The compatibility rule is shared by booking and continued bed access. Bed choice gives non-partners last priority; it uses no job-context flag or additional saved state. The implementation and its documentation were reviewed after the passing DLC and Core runs.

An earlier refactor moved sleep jobs to vanilla `LayDown` and `JobDriver_LayDown`. The custom job definition, driver and DefOf class have been removed. The job factory creates ordinary sleep jobs; selection remains stable while travelling by consulting the bed comp's assignment. The reservation postfix runs only after a successful vanilla reservation and only when the pawn has a temporary assignment. It then validates the assigned bed without making another reservation. At that stage night visits also used `LayDown`; the current think-tree implementation uses `Lovin`, as described below.

The regression suite checks the job definition and exact vanilla driver type, a replacement vanilla job retaining its bed reservation and temporary slot after the original booking job releases its reservations, ordinary bed sleep without a temporary assignment, ordinary ground sleep, and preservation of a native reservation failure without releasing a different booking. Existing tests still exercise visits, queued reservations, cancellation, invalid occupants, relationship changes, and lovin' through completion. After medical conversion ends an assignment, the job follows ordinary vanilla reservation behavior; that test retains its assignment cleanup and ownership assertions.

The initial reservation-failure fixture used a second ordinary reservation, but vanilla accepts an already-held reservation before checking other ordinary reservations. Inspection of `ReservationManager.CanReserve` established that behavior. The corrected fixture uses the physical-interaction reservation that vanilla checks first, and asserts the native reservation conflict before testing the sleep job. The failed fixture's report is in `temp/Yobai-vanilla-sleep-dlc-*`.

One DLC run timed out in the existing `OccupiedUnassignedSlotDoesNotPermitVisitToAssignedSlot` case: the intended host had left the bed and was building a roof by teardown. Its report is `temp/Yobai-vanilla-sleep-verified-dlc-Test.log`. The test's assertions were preserved. A full diagnostic run passed all 61 cases, and 12 temporary repetitions of that case also passed; their reports are in `temp/Yobai-vanilla-sleep-diagnostic-dlc-*` and `temp/Yobai-vanilla-sleep-host-stress-*`. The temporary repetitions and logging were removed. Native job replacement during the sleep setup is a possible cause, but the failed transition was not captured, so this remains an unresolved intermittent test failure. No runtime workaround or fixture-specific behavior was added.

Map-cell reservations have been removed from temporary bed sharing. For sleep jobs, the factory reserves only bed capacity; the vanilla sleep postfix validates the comp's assignment without reserving again. Temporary sleepers still get distinct slots through the comp. The sleeping-place value now contains only the bed and slot, and the redundant reservation helper was removed.

The cell-reservation regression reserves slot zero's map cell for another job, then checks that a temporary sleeper can book slot zero, pass vanilla job reservation, reach that exact sleeping position, and retain their original owned bed. The other job keeps its cell reservation. Existing continuation, rollback and Scribe cases now check that the bed reservation and comp assignment survive without cell reservations. Ordinary ground sleep still reserves its cell through vanilla behavior.

Bedroom classification now uses vanilla's love cluster without an override. `SpouseRelationUtility.GetLoveCluster` already traverses the living spouse, fiancé and lover relationships transitively, including relationships whose ideoligions prohibit physical intimacy. The replacement with Yobai's stricter polycule only narrowed vanilla's accepted bedroom groups and has been removed. All added room-role invalidation hooks have also been removed, along with their helper. Vanilla manages both classification and caching; Yobai computes its physical-partner polycule afresh.

Three new ideology regressions preserve vanilla bedrooms for prohibited spouses and for lovers or fiancés under strict spouse-only precepts. They also check that those relationships still do not connect Yobai's physical-partner polycule or grant temporary bed sharing. All three failed with the old override, reporting `Bedroom` expected but `Barracks` actual; the baseline report is in `temp/Yobai-vanilla-bedroom-baseline-*`. After removing the override, the complete DLC suite passed 65 tests. The three cases were then strengthened to force a fresh room-role calculation instead of accepting a cached result, and passed again in `temp/Yobai-vanilla-bedroom-fresh-*`. Existing assertions were preserved.

The breakup, parenthood and resurrection cases now explicitly notify the room before checking its recalculated role. Their bedroom/barracks assertions are unchanged. They no longer require Yobai to invalidate vanilla's cached role automatically. The resurrection case additionally checks the physical-partner polycule immediately after death and resurrection, independently of the room refresh.

Bed access now checks the sleeping place the pawn would actually use for both permanent owners and temporary sleepers. The initial owner-occupancy regression failed before the fix; its baseline is in `temp/Yobai-owner-slot-baseline-*`. The first fix passed 66 tests but checked only physical occupants, leaving booked arrivals unprotected.

The comp now derives a shared view of sleeping places from occupants, temporary assignments, and owners' native bed reservations for sleep jobs. A temporary assignment overrides the same owner's permanent position. Repair and other work reservations do not count as incoming sleepers. Assignment, bed access, compatibility, continuation, bedtime preference, and a host's decision to leave all use that view. Owners starting vanilla sleep jobs are validated against existing temporary stays before their new reservation can block those stays.

Seven arrival regressions cover both booking orders, the owner's free alternative slot, cancellation, incoming-owner compatibility, unrelated work reservations, relationship changes before arrival, incoming-partner preference, and a host waiting for an incoming partner. Six failed with the occupancy-only fix; the work-reservation case already passed. Their baseline is in `temp/Yobai-incoming-slots-baseline-*`. The full DLC run passed all 73 cases in `temp/Yobai-incoming-slots-dlc-*`; Core alone passed all 64 cases in `temp/Yobai-incoming-slots-core-*`. Both games exited with code 0.

The existing occupied-booking case now moves a sleeper from its admitted slot into the booked slot to simulate an unexpected occupant. Normal owner admission must reject that collision rather than create it. Its bed-access, reservation-failure, cleanup and night-visit assertions are preserved. The existing relationship-change case now changes the relationship after the first pawn arrives; a separate case checks rejection while both are still travelling. The returning-owner preference test now expects the occupied permanent slot to be unavailable while retaining its checks that the pawn chooses another bed and keeps permanent ownership.

Night visits now run from an XML-added `JobGiver_NightVisit` in the lying-down branch of the humanlike think tree. The job giver combines target selection, both pawns' lovin' cooldowns, mutual reservation checks, the game's partner-specific lovin' MTB, and a deterministic hourly roll. It issues ordinary `Lovin` jobs, followed by ordinary sleep after arrival. The four-hour map timer and the separate bedtime visit roll have been removed. The old map component is an empty compatibility type for already-saved maps. Cancelled comp assignments are pruned on lookup and before saving.

The initial approach added the arrival condition to every temporary lovin' job. Three existing lovin' regressions failed because job startup can reset posture even when the pawn is already at its sleeping place. The check now applies only when the bed toil begins a journey from another position, leaving ordinary in-bed lovin' checks unchanged. Those failures are recorded in `TestResults/Yobai/ThinkTree Initial-20261020`.

The first expanded suite passed 80 of 81 cases. The remaining new cancellation test waited for the `Job` object reference to change, which is unsafe because `Pawn_JobTracker.CleanupCurrentJob` returns jobs to `JobMaker`'s pool. Its log showed the pawn already wandering while the wait timed out. The test now captures the job's numeric ID and retains all cancellation, reservation and ownership assertions. This failed run is in `TestResults/Yobai/ThinkTree DLC-20261020`. A subsequent run passed all 82 cases before the final breakup regression was added.

Vanilla's `KeepLyingDown` queues sleep at the lovin' destination even if a travelling visit fails. With Yobai's conditional continuation temporarily disabled, both cancellation cases failed and the pawn had a queued or active sleep job at that destination. The baseline is in `TestResults/Yobai/ThinkTree Cancellation Baseline-20261020`. The restored patch queues sleep only after the visitor reaches the bed. The arrival checks also reject a breakup even when an indirect polycule connection still allows the pair to share that bed.

The final think-tree implementation passed all **83 DLC cases** and **74 Core cases**, with both games exiting successfully. Reports are in `TestResults/Yobai/ThinkTree Final DLC-20261020` and `ThinkTree Final Core-20261020`. Ten new cases cover automatic think-tree dispatch through lovin' completion and sleep, each partner's cooldown, the hourly limit, starvation, travel cancellation for a departed or unavailable partner, a breakup within a still-connected polycule, reservation rollback, and removal of cancelled assignments before saving. The existing bedtime case now requires zero visits. Existing room-role, sharing, ownership, lovin' penalty and continuation assertions remain covered. After the final helper rename and guard cleanup, four focused cases passed again: automatic think-tree visits, both travel-cancellation paths, and ordinary in-bed lovin'. Their report is in `TestResults/Yobai/ThinkTree Final Review-20261020`.

Night visits also support beds with more than two sleeping places. Selection considers every eligible sleeping host in a bed, while compatibility checks include all occupants and booked arrivals. A host on cooldown cannot hide another ready host in the same bed. Visits still need an unassigned place that is neither occupied nor booked.

Five new regression cases use an XML-defined three-place bed in the test mod, without requiring an external bed mod. They cover both host positions with the other host on cooldown, lovin' and sleep continuation with a third occupant, incompatible occupants and incoming owners, and two visitors competing for the last unassigned place. All five passed in `TestResults/Yobai/Three Slot Focused-20261020`. The full DLC suite then passed all **88 cases** in `TestResults/Yobai/Three Slot Full DLC-20261020`. The full Core-only suite passed all **79 cases** in `TestResults/Yobai/Three Slot Full Core-20261020`. Larger beds supplied by other mods have not been tested.

Owner-slot preference was checked with eight new regression cases. With the previous chooser, the second and third owners of an empty three-place bed both received slot zero; those two cases failed, while the first owner's case and the occupied/booked fallback cases passed. This baseline is in `TestResults/Yobai/Owner Slot Baseline-20261020`.

Bedroom selection now tries an owner's permanently assigned place first, then the other available places. The initial implementation also preferred that place for returning night visitors, but unnecessarily required an additional free unassigned place. That restriction and the regression enforcing it have since been corrected: owners can use their own free place, including in fully assigned beds, and only borrowing another place requires it to be unassigned. The new tests check all three owner positions before travel and after arrival, fallback for occupants and booked arrivals, returning-owner visits, and preservation of the visit eligibility rule. The full DLC suite passed all **96 cases** in `TestResults/Yobai/Owner Slot Full DLC-20261020`, with no failures. Permanent ownership assertions remain covered.

The corrected night-visit rule passed the full **98-case DLC suite** in `TestResults/Yobai/Returning Owner Corrected-20261020`. The former spare-place regression now verifies that an owner can use their own free place while the unassigned place is booked. Two additional cases cover returning from another bed to a fully assigned double bed, the owner's actual sleeping position, the toggle, and rejection when another sleeper has booked the owner's place. Guests still cannot borrow an absent owner's place. Fully assigned beds now show the toggle when an owner has a partner assigned elsewhere, so the existing guest-rejection test retains its admission assertions and expects the toggle to be available.

The serialized comp keys and assignment fields are unchanged. [Design notes](../Yobai/DESIGN.md) explain the responsibilities of selection, assignment storage, and job reservations.
