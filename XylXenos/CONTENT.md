## Xenotypes

### Major xenotypes

Major xenotypes are the main content of XylXenos. They have dedicated factions and events and unique mechanics. They are more common and have more unique traits than minor xenotypes.

* Bossaps: Sentient livestock. They're hardy and do well in large colonies, but have a chance of going into an uncontrollable rage if damaged in combat.
* Chyrr: Nocturnal flyers. Their wings and innate stun ability make them excellent skirmishers, but they go into hibernation in lower temperatures.
* Dvergr: Industrious dwarves. Great workers and craftspeople, but they have a genetic dependence on alcohol and tend to get into fights and throw tantrums.
* Nixie: Psychic fish people. They're unmatched in water, but unless they periodically soak to keep their skin moist they'll become very unhappy.
* Titan: Engineered rock giants. They're super tough and each is specialized in one random skill, but they move slowly and have difficulty learning other skills. They can't use most normal drugs and require special drugs designed for lithoids.
* Trog: Adaptable mutants. They can live almost anywhere and wild insects won't attack them, but they're slow learners and sensitive to sunlight.
* Warcat: Carnivorous hunters. They're ferocious melee fighters with a rage ability that lets them move and attack incredibly quickly, but they need to eat raw meat regularly to survive.
* Zeegee: High-tech spacers. They learn fast and are excellent shots with long-ranged weapons, but they do poorly on planetary surfaces.

### Minor xenotypes

Minor xenotypes are found as rare members of other factions or in random events. They have fewer unique traits and mechanics than major xenotypes.

* Omegabeaver: Semi-aquatic builders. They move fast in water and are great at logging and construction, but poor at combat.
* Scaleborn: Draconic warriors. Each scaleborn comes from one of five lineages with a different breath ability. They're powerful at combat, but hard to keep happy.
* Steadling: Hardy generalists. They're adaptable and do well in early stage colonies, but they have a genetic dependence on smokeleaf.
* Succuboid: Charming seducers. They can give their romantic partners a huge mood boost, but intimacy with one is neurochemically addictive. They're all but useless at work and combat.

* Menagerie: Crashlanded, but any character can be any non-archite xenotype.

## Incidents and Quests

* A Titanic Problem: A fugitive titan and a load of stolen goods, chased by mechanoids.
* Wild bossaps herd: 2-4 tameable bossaps wild people.
* Zeegee pod crash: A zeegee refugee crashes on a space platform. Requires Odyssey.

## Items

### Drugs

* Softener: Lithoid only. Prevents petrification; no effect on an existing disease.
* Atlasite: Lithoid only. Provides slight boosts to mood, movement, and manipulation. Gives resistance to hostile environments that increases with repeated doses over time but resets on a missed dose.
* Crystal: Lithoid only. Increases mood and consciousness and reduces pain. Highly addictive; high tolerance may cause chemical damage to the brain.

### Materials

* Coral: Beautiful stone with decent stats. Available from nixie traders.
* Sea silk: Beautiful cloth with excellent protection and cold insulation. Available from nixie bulk goods traders.

### Buildings

* Shower: Allows nixies to meet their wetness need without access to water. Only buildable by nixies.

## Traits

* Flirt/Don Juan: Characters with this trait are much more likely to attempt to romance other characters. Also unlocks the romantic meditation type, which allows using a double bed as a meditation focus.

## Unique mechanics

* Genetic recipe and building unlocks: Genes can unlock unique recipes and buildings or ones which otherwise require a specific meme. Some unlocks require Ideology.
* Psycast genes: Every psycast gets an associated gene, which grants the ability to use the psycast without a psylink. Requires Royalty.
* Xenotype meme compatibility: Each xenotype has favored and disfavored memes, which affects conversion speeed. Requires Ideology.
