## Xenotypes

### Major xenotypes

Major xenotypes are the main content of XylXenos. They have dedicated factions and events and unique mechanics. They are more common and have more unique traits than minor xenotypes.

#### Bossaps

Sentient livestock. They're hardy and do well in large colonies, but have a chance of going into an uncontrollable rage if damaged in combat.

Bossaps' gene package makes them easy to feed, easy to keep as slaves, robust to temperatures, and good in melee combat. Their main disadvantage is their rage.

#### Chyrr

Nocturnal flyers. Their wings and innate stun ability make them excellent skirmishers, but they go into hibernation in lower temperatures.

Chyrr are well adapted for warm climates and nocturnal activity. Their flight and stun abilities make them great for hit-and-run attacks, but they don't do well in a straight-up fight. Using them in cold climates is a challenge, espcially when caravanning. Their echolocation ability makes them uniquely compatible with the Blindsight meme.

#### Dvergr

Industrious dwarves. Great workers and craftspeople, but they have a genetic dependence on alcohol and tend to get into fights and throw tantrums.

Dvergr's gene set focuses on labor, with bonuses to global work speed and to construction, mining, and crafting. They're well suited to life underground living off fungus. They're prone to mood problems, though, and require keeping up a steady supply of alcohol.

#### Nixie

Psychic fish people. They're unmatched in water, but unless they periodically soak to keep their skin moist they'll become very unhappy.

Nixies do best in cold maps with lots of water. They're poor at melee, but specialize at using Beckon to lure enemies into water where they can swim circles around them. Their main drawback is they need to spend hours a day soaking in water. The amount of soaking they need climbs rapidly as temperatures go up.

#### Titan

Engineered rock giants. They're super tough and each is specialized in one random skill, but they move slowly and have difficulty learning other skills. They can't use most normal drugs and require special drugs designed for lithoids.

Titans' gene package is focused on one thing: toughness. They have extra HP, their skin provides natural armor that reduces damage, bleeding wounds close automatically, and they shrug off infections and are completely immune to toxins. However, if they do get injured, they will spend a long time recovering. Labor-wise, each Titan is particularly good at one thing, but generally terrible at anything else. Their unique drugs also provide a special challenge to supply.

#### Trog

Adaptable mutants. They can live almost anywhere and wild insects won't attack them, but they're slow learners and sensitive to sunlight.

Trogs' genome seems at first glance to be nothing but disadvantages: ugly, slow moving, bad learners, and sensitive to sunlight. Somehow, though, they turn out to be remarkably good survivors. Each one has a 50% chance of having a random xenogene, which can make them surprising enemies in combat. As colonists, their biggest advantage is that they aren't attacked by wild insects, allowing them to tame them risk-free.

#### Warcat

Graceful predators. They're powerful melee fighters with a rage ability that lets them move and attack incredibly quickly, but they need to eat raw meat regularly to survive.

Warcats' gene package is focused heavily on melee combat. They're not great workers, mainly because of their need for extra sleep. Keeping them supplied with fresh meat is the biggest challenge. In the early game, you'll probably need to get most of your food by hunting - although they're always happy to eat the corpses of your enemies, too.

#### Zeegee

High-tech spacers. They learn fast and are excellent shots with long-ranged weapons, but they do poorly on planetary surfaces.

Zeegees can be challenging to play. They're excellent in space, with the ability to survive in vacuum completely naked for up to 24 hours. They also make excellent mechanitors. However, as ordinary colonists they're fragile and prone to bouts of debilitating planet sickness. In combat they make excellent snipers but terrible melee fighters.

### Minor xenotypes

Minor xenotypes are found as rare members of other factions or in random events. They have fewer unique traits and mechanics than major xenotypes.

#### Omegabeaver

Semi-aquatic builders. They move fast in water and are great at logging and construction, but poor at combat.

Omegabeavers are excellent workers, easy to keep happy, and cute mascots for the rest of your colony. They're also absolutely terrible at combat.

#### Scaleborn

Draconic warriors. Each scaleborn comes from one of five lineages with a different breath ability. They're powerful at combat, but hard to keep happy.

Scaleborn's main gimmick is their five lineages, each with its own unique strengths. They're tough and good at combat, but their innate Greedy trait makes them hard to keep happy. Like warcats, early on you'll probably have to get food for them by hunting.

#### Steadling

Hardy generalists. They're adaptable and do well in early stage colonies, but they have a genetic dependence on smokeleaf.

Steadlings are the perfect early game colonists, able to adapt to most environments and do many different jobs. However, they don't do as well in large colonies.

#### Succuboid

Charming seductresses. They can give their romantic partners a huge mood boost, but intimacy with one is neurochemically addictive. They're all but useless at work and combat.

The succuboid gene package is laser focused on romance. Their love euphoria ability gives a huge mood boost to their lovers, but a breakup means a long period of withdrawal. They're almost completely useless at labor, and not particularly good at combat.

## Genes

Includes 121 total genes:

* 73 non-cosmetic genes with a wide range of effects, from wings that allow actual flight to pheromones that stop wild insects from attacking. Many of these have cosmetic effects, too.
* 29 psycast genes that grant the ability to use a specific psycast without a psylink. Requires Royalty.
* 6 unique cosmetic genes.
* 6 sex-linked hair genes.
* 7 new skin color genes.

## Factions

* Rough dvergr union: Dvergr, *dirtmole, baseliner,* titan, *hussar,* trog, *genie, impid, neanderthal, pigskin, waster, yttakin,* omegabeaver
* Gentle nixie tribe: Nixie
* Cannibal warcat tribe: Warcat
* Savage trog tribe: Trog, *dirtmole, neanderthal, waster,* bossaps, dvergr, nixie, warcat, *baseliner, impid, pigskin, yttakin,* chyrr, scaleborn

## Scenarios

* Dvergr Fortress: Seven dwarves and a lot of booze. Strike the earth!
* Menagerie: Crashlanded, but any character can be any non-archite xenotype.
* The Siren: One nixie with noncombat psychic abilities. Requires Royalty.
* Warcat Tribe: Three warcats and two bossaps slaves.

## Incidents and Quests

* A Titanic Problem: A fugitive titan and a load of stolen goods, chased by mechanoids.
* Wild bossaps herd: 2-4 tameable bossaps wild people.
* Zeegee pod crash: A zeegee refugee crashes on a space platform. Requires Odyssey.

## Items

### Drugs

* Softener: Lithoid only. Prevents petrification; no effect on an existing disease.
* Atlasite: Lithoid only. Provides slight boosts to mood, movement, and manipulation. Gives resistance to hostile environments that increases with repeated doses over time but resets on a missed dose.
* Crystal: Lithoid only. Increases mood and consciousness and reduces pain. Highly addictive; high tolerance may cause chemical damage to the brain.

### Materials

* Coral: Beautiful stone with decent stats. Available from nixie traders.
* Sea silk: Beautiful cloth with excellent protection and cold insulation. Available from nixie bulk goods traders.

### Buildings

* Shower: Allows nixies to meet their wetness need without access to water. Only buildable by nixies.

## Traits

* Flirt/Don Juan: Characters with this trait are much more likely to attempt to romance other characters. Also unlocks the romantic meditation type, which allows using a double bed as a meditation focus.

## Animals

* Selkie: Genetically engineered seals used by nixie traders as pack animals. Requires Odyssey.
* Shellback: A type of giant insect that can appear during raids by the savage trog tribe. They can take a lot of damage, and attack with a single powerful horn.

## Unique mechanics

* Genetic recipe and building unlocks: Genes can unlock unique recipes and buildings or ones which otherwise require a specific meme. Some unlocks require Ideology.
* Xenotype meme compatibility: Each xenotype has favored and disfavored memes, which affects conversion speeed. Requires Ideology.

## Configuration

* Xenotype selection: You can enable and disable xenotypes, even base game ones, in the mod settings. This is safe to do in an existing save but won't change characters or factions already in the game. Requires restart to take effect.
