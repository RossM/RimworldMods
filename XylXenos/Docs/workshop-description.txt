Yep, it's yet another gene and xenotype mod for RimWorld. But this one is mine, and I'm very proud of it. So [i]nyah.[/i] Download it and give it a try, it would make me happy. It would also make me happy if you give it a thumbs up if you like it.

This mod includes 12 xenotypes, 121 new genes, 4 factions, 5 scenarios, and a variety of new events, animals, buildings, and items. Everything is designed and balanced to be lore-friendly and fit with vanilla RimWorld as well as I can make it.

Since this is an initial release, and finding good playtesters is [i]hard,[/i] there's probably a lot of rough edges. Want to be a playtester? Just play and report your thoughts and experiences in the comments section. It really helps! There should be dedicated threads for bug reports, balance feedback, gameplay stories, and mod compatibility reports/requests. If there aren't I forgot to make them, oops.

[b]AI disclosure:[/b] AI was used in minor ways while developing this mod, primarily for writing tests and auxiliary scripts and build management and other boring stuff. AI generated content was used during iteration on some ideas but the final results are all mine.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

RimWorld 1.7 and Biotech are required.

Optional DLC unlocks additional content:

[list]
[*] Royalty: Innate psycast genes and a nixie-specific scenario.
[*] Ideology: Integration with the ideoligeon system and additional gene-based unlocks for some Ideology content that's normally locked to specific memes.
[*] Odyssey: Additional space-related content and a variant seal that nixie traders will use as pack animals.
[/list]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[h2]Can I add this to an existing save?[/h2]

Yes. Some content, such as the factions, requires a new save to see.

[h2]Can I remove it from an existing save?[/h2]

Not recommended. Let me know if it works.

[h2]How do I find the xenotypes and acquire their genes?[/h2]

Bossaps, chyrr, dvergr, nixie, titan, trog, warcat, and zeegee are what I consider "major" xenotypes. Most of them have their own factions or events to introduce them, and all of them appear fairly frequently in appropriate factions. You shouldn't have any trouble finding them during play, except maybe chyrr, which are intentionally a bit rarer.

The other four xenotypes - omegabeaver, scaleborn, steadling, and succuboid - are "minor" xenotypes. You might rarely see one during play, but your best bet is to pick one as a starting colonist.

Most of the genes can also be found in gene packs available from traders.

[h2]I'm a mod author. Can I use your stuff in my mod?[/h2]

Sure. You can depend on my mod, or you can use my code or assets as a start for your own - it's all MIT licensed. If you'd like to use the code that powers the mod without pulling in all the gameplay content, I'm considering making it available as a standalone library mod; let me know if you want that to happen.
